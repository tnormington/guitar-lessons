About

Hey, I'm Tim.

I'm not a full-time music teacher. I'm a dad, a creative person, and someone who has loved playing guitar for years. I get how intimidating it can feel to start -- that's exactly why I teach the way I do.

No music theory lectures. No boring exercises that feel like homework. From lesson one, we're working toward songs you actually want to play.

I teach out of Dover, can come to you, or we can do it online. Evenings and weekends work for me, because they probably work better for you too.

Process

How It Works

  1. 01

    Book a Free Intro

    Shoot me an email, we'll set up a free 30-minute intro lesson. No pressure, just guitar. We'll go over what you want to learn and figure out if we're a good fit.

  2. 02

    Start Learning

    We set a regular schedule that works for you -- evenings, weekends, whatever fits. Each lesson builds on the last, at your pace, not some preset curriculum.

  3. 03

    Play Songs You Love

    Within a month, you'll be playing recognizable songs. That's the goal. Keep going and you'll be surprising yourself within a few months.
